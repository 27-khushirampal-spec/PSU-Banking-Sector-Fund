# Fundamental, Technical Analysis and Construction of a PSU Banking Sector Fund

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on the **fundamental analysis of the Indian Public Sector Banking (PSU Banking) sector** and the construction of a hypothetical sector-focused fund.

The analysis covers **12 PSU banking companies** and evaluates their financial performance, valuation, banking-specific financial ratios and relative attractiveness. Based on the analysis, companies were ranked and used for the construction of a hypothetical PSU Banking Sector Fund.

The project also includes **sector index construction, NAV calculation, portfolio allocation and technical analysis components**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The key objectives of the project were to:

- Analyse the financial and fundamental performance of selected PSU banks
- Compare companies using banking-specific financial parameters
- Evaluate relative valuation across the selected companies
- Identify relatively attractive and over/undervalued companies
- Develop a ranking framework for company selection
- Construct a custom PSU Banking Sector Index
- Calculate the NAV of the hypothetical sector fund
- Develop an asset allocation strategy for the fund
- Analyse selected technical indicators across different trading horizons

---

## 📊 Fundamental Analysis

The fundamental analysis focuses on financial and banking-specific indicators such as:

### Profitability & Performance
- Return on Assets (ROA)
- Net Interest Margin (NIM)
- Profitability trends
- Earnings performance

### Asset Quality
- Gross NPA
- Asset quality indicators

### Capital Strength
- Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR)

### Valuation
- Price-to-Earnings (P/E)
- Price-to-Book (P/B)
- PEG Ratio
- Relative valuation analysis

These parameters were used to compare the selected PSU banks and assess their relative financial strength and valuation attractiveness.

---

## 📈 Company Ranking & Selection

A comparative ranking framework was developed using selected fundamental and valuation parameters.

The objective was to identify companies that demonstrated relatively stronger fundamentals and more attractive valuation characteristics within the PSU Banking sector.

The analysis also included identification of companies classified as relatively **overvalued or undervalued** based on the selected valuation framework.

---

## 📐 PSU Banking Sector Index

A custom **PSU Banking Sector Index** was constructed using a market-capitalisation-based approach.

The index methodology involved:

1. Selection of the PSU banking constituents
2. Collection of market capitalisation data
3. Calculation of constituent weights
4. Construction of the sector index
5. Tracking of index-level performance

---

## 💰 Hypothetical PSU Banking Sector Fund

Based on the fundamental analysis and company ranking, a hypothetical PSU Banking Sector Fund was constructed.

### Assumed AUM

**₹10 Crore**

The fund construction involved:

- Selection of eligible companies
- Ranking of companies
- Determination of portfolio weights
- Asset allocation
- Calculation of investment amounts
- Evaluation of the resulting portfolio

The objective was to translate the fundamental analysis into a practical **sector-focused investment portfolio**.
